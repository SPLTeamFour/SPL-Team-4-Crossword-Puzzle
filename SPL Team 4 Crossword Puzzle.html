How to play

  1. Tap a cell or a clue to start. Tap the same cell again to switch between across and down.
  2. Type letters. On a phone the keyboard opens when you tap a cell.
  3. Use ◀ ▶ above the grid to jump between clues.
  4. Stuck? Press Hint for one letter or Reveal word.
  5. Press Check to mark wrong letters in red, or tick Show mistakes as I type.
  6. Use + / − to zoom the grid. Your progress saves automatically.
